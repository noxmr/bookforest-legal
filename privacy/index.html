Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026
The essentials: BookForest asks for no account, name, email or phone number. Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone: we run no servers of our own and never receive your data. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking. The app goes online only to look up books and covers on Open Library and to handle purchases through Apple.

Data that stays on your device

BookForest is an iPhone app that tracks your reading and turns it into worlds that grow with every session. It stores locally, in the app's own storage:

  • The books you add: title, author, page count, genre, world, current page and the address of the cover image.
  • Your reading sessions (date, duration and pages read) and any notes you write about each book.
  • Your preferences: session length, daily and yearly goals, reminder time, night mode and tutorial progress.
  • If you have BookForest Premium, a local flag remembering that it is unlocked.

The app does not send this data off your device. It may be included in your iPhone backups (for example in iCloud) if you have them enabled; those backups are handled by Apple under its own policy. Deleting the app deletes its data from the device.

Book and cover lookup (Open Library)

When you type a title while adding a book, the app queries Open Library, a service of the Internet Archive, to suggest the title, author, page count and cover. Cover images are also downloaded from its servers whenever the app displays them.

In those requests Open Library receives the text you searched for and, like any website, technical connection data such as your IP address. BookForest sends no identifier for you or your device along with the search. The Internet Archive's handling of that data is governed by its privacy policy.

If you would rather not use the lookup, you can enter the book details by hand.

Purchases and subscriptions

BookForest Premium (monthly, yearly or one-time purchase) is bought through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment with your Apple account: we never see or store your card, name or email. The app only receives confirmation from Apple of whether the purchase is active. For Apple's privacy policy, see apple.com/legal/privacy.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks the system for permission to show notifications. They are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone and never pass through any server of ours. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings.

What we don't do

  • We collect no personal or usage data.
  • We use no analytics or advertising services.
  • We share or sell nothing: there is nothing to share.
  • We neither require nor offer account creation.

Children

BookForest can be used by readers of any age. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the current version will always live at this page, with its effective date.
